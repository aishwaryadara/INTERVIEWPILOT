# User Journey

## Primary Flow

1. The interviewer logs in.
2. The interviewer opens the dashboard.
3. The interviewer creates a candidate profile.
4. The interviewer schedules an interview.
5. The interviewer opens the interview room.
6. The interviewer confirms candidate consent for transcription or recording.
7. The interview starts.
8. Transcript segments appear during the conversation.
9. AI suggests follow-up questions based on the conversation context.
10. The interviewer writes notes and marks rubric evidence.
11. The interviewer ends the interview.
12. AI generates a structured summary and scoring suggestions.
13. The interviewer reviews and edits the report.
14. The final report becomes available to the hiring team.

## Key Screens

- Login
- Register
- Dashboard
- Candidate list
- Candidate profile
- Schedule interview
- Interview room
- Interview report
- Settings

## Interview Room Layout

- Candidate summary panel.
- Live transcript panel.
- Suggested follow-up questions.
- Interviewer private notes.
- Rubric and competency scoring panel.
- Session timer and controls.
