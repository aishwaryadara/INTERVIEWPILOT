# User Personas

## Technical Interviewer

### Goals

- Run a focused interview.
- Ask useful follow-up questions.
- Capture evidence without losing the conversation.
- Submit a clear interview report quickly.

### Pain Points

- Hard to listen, think, and write at the same time.
- Follow-up quality varies by interviewer experience.
- Notes often become vague after the interview.
- Rubric scoring can feel subjective.

## Hiring Manager

### Goals

- Compare candidates consistently.
- Review interview evidence quickly.
- Reduce noisy or biased feedback.
- Make better hiring decisions with structured data.

### Pain Points

- Interview feedback is inconsistent.
- Some interviewers submit weak summaries.
- Hiring committee discussions lack evidence.
- Competency signals are scattered across notes.

## Recruiter

### Goals

- Track interview progress.
- Share useful summaries with the hiring team.
- Keep candidates moving through the process.

### Pain Points

- Interview feedback arrives late.
- Reports are hard to interpret.
- Hiring teams use inconsistent language.

## Engineering Manager

### Goals

- Understand candidate strengths and risks.
- Evaluate role fit.
- Identify signal quality across interview loops.

### Pain Points

- Interview feedback can be too opinion-based.
- Strong and weak signals are not clearly separated.
- Candidate comparisons are time-consuming.
